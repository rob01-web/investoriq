# 1. PRODUCT PURPOSE

Full Underwriting must be a decision document that helps a serious investor, lender, acquisitions team, asset manager, or investment committee understand:

1. what the property is,
2. what the evidence actually proves,
3. what the operating economics show,
4. what transaction terms imply,
5. what the major risks and constraints are,
6. what variables matter most,
7. how supported sensitivities change outcomes,
8. what remains unresolved,
9. what diligence would materially improve conviction,
10. and why Full Underwriting delivers materially more decision value than Screening.

This is not a page-count exercise.

There is no arbitrary 14-page cap. Report length should follow evidence and decision usefulness.

Guiding principle:

> **Better analysis may derive more insight from canonical facts, but it may never pretend the evidence is stronger than the uploaded/adjudicated sources support.**

# 5. ELITE-10 VISUAL SYSTEM TARGETS

## Typography
- institutional rather than dashboard-like
- restrained headline scale
- readable body copy
- consistent numeric styles
- clear distinction between chapter, section, subsection, table label, note, and disclosure

## Color
- restrained palette
- use contrast to signal hierarchy, not decoration
- reserve strong emphasis for decision-critical content
- avoid excessive status colors

## Page geometry
- intentional margins
- repeatable chapter spacing
- stable table widths
- predictable page breaks
- no orphaned section headings
- no awkward single-row table continuations where avoidable

## Tables
- compact
- aligned
- obvious units
- consistent negative/positive presentation
- minimal border noise
- header hierarchy that survives PDF rendering
- no duplicate data just to fill space

## Callouts
Use only when they improve decisions:
- primary constraint
- material diligence item
- valuation discrepancy
- debt coverage issue
- evidence limitation
- quality incident

## Charts
Use only where a chart does more than repeat a table:
- scenario sensitivity
- driver ranking
- operating mix/variance
- selected debt/valuation relationships

## Disclosures
Primary pages should not read like legal/debug output.
Keep disclosures compact and human-readable, while preserving detailed source/certification evidence in the Source Appendix / Quality Manifest.
