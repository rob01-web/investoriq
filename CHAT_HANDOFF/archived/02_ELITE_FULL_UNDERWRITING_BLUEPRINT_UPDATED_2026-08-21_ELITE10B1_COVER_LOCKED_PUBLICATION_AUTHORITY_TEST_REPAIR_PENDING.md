## 1. Product purpose

Full Underwriting must help a serious investor, lender, acquisitions team, asset manager, or investment committee understand:

1. what the property is,
2. what the evidence proves,
3. what the operating economics show,
4. what the transaction and debt terms imply,
5. what the major risks and constraints are,
6. which variables matter most,
7. how supported sensitivities change outcomes,
8. what remains unresolved,
9. what diligence would improve conviction,
10. why Full Underwriting provides materially more decision value than Screening.

This is not a page-count exercise. Report length follows evidence and decision usefulness. No arbitrary page cap and no filler.

Guiding principle:

> Better analysis may derive more insight from canonical facts, but it may never pretend the evidence is stronger than the uploaded and adjudicated sources support.

## 6. ELITE-10 visual system

### Design principles

- restrained institutional language,
- strong chapter and page hierarchy,
- disciplined typography,
- generous but efficient whitespace,
- high decision density,
- fewer decorative dashboard cards,
- compact, quickly scannable tables,
- clear separation of evidence, deterministic analysis, scenarios, and unresolved diligence,
- charts only where they add decision value,
- consistent numeric alignment, units, periods, and labels,
- clean print-safe page breaks,
- compact disclosures on primary pages,
- detailed evidence and certification retained in the appendix.

### Typography

- institutional rather than dashboard-like,
- restrained headline scale,
- readable body copy,
- consistent numeric styles,
- clear chapter, section, subsection, table, note, and disclosure hierarchy.

### Tables and charts

- obvious units and time periods,
- consistent positive and negative presentation,
- minimal border noise,
- stable widths and headers,
- no duplicated data to fill space,
- no chart that merely repeats a table.

### Callouts

Use only for a material constraint, diligence item, valuation discrepancy, coverage issue, evidence limitation, or quality incident.
