## Product Direction

InvestorIQ has two distinct report products:

- Screening: rapid capital triage from limited accepted evidence.
- Underwriting: institutional, source-aware acquisition and capital-risk analysis.

The immediate product objective is Premium Acquisition Underwriting V1. It must materially deepen the current Underwriting Report without reopening the stabilized pipeline.

## Current Priority: Premium Acquisition Underwriting V1

### Product outcome

Produce an underwriting artifact suitable for serious acquisition, financing, and investment-committee review by using canonical accepted evidence comprehensively and presenting authorized deterministic analysis clearly.

### Architecture

- Additive consume-only sidecar.
- Default-off capability flag.
- Immutable job-level surface version before external launch.
- Direct canonical-receipt inputs.
- Independent validation and certification.
- One guarded renderer insertion.
- Instant rollback.

### Content outcome

When supported, the premium report should contain:

- Executive underwriting conclusions grounded in accepted evidence.
- Property and transaction context.
- Detailed operating performance.
- Rent Roll and unit economics.
- Expense structure and per-unit analysis.
- Current and proposed debt comparison.
- Coverage, debt yield, leverage, and debt-service burden.
- Valuation and appraisal bridge.
- Capital-plan evidence.
- Market and environmental evidence.
- Evidence and diligence register.
- Source reconciliation.
- Methods, definitions, limitations, and supporting appendices.

There is no fixed page target. Every page must earn its place.

## Protected Foundation

The Premium V1 program does not initially change:

- Source Truth.
- Core Publish-or-Collapse.
- Delivery Gate source eligibility.
- Worker orchestration.
- Publication and storage authority.
- Billing, credits, remedies, or customer lifecycle.
- Screening.
- PDF Boss doctrine.

## Product Guardrails

Never use report length as a substitute for analysis.

Never add:

- Filler narrative.
- Duplicated Screening content.
- Unsupported market claims.
- Hardcoded refinance assumptions.
- IRR, DCF, growth, discount, exit, or return analysis without authority.
- Renovation value creation without authority.
- BUY/SELL or equivalent recommendation language.
- Customer-visible implementation terminology.

Never silently deliver a base artifact for an externally promised premium job that failed premium certification.

## Near-Term Definition of Success

The next release is successful when:

- Accepted source evidence is visibly and analytically utilized.
- The report has financing-grade operating, debt, valuation, and diligence depth.
- Sparse inputs collapse honestly without making the whole report look broken.
- Rich inputs produce rich output without padding.
- The PDF is dense, readable, and compositionally sound.
- The protected pipeline remains unchanged when premium is disabled.
- Premium certification is independent from core source delivery eligibility.

That is the launch standard.
